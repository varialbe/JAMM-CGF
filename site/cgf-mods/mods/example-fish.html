What it does

Goldie is a small, shallow-water fish found near the dock. Glowfin is a medium nocturnal fish that pulses cyan and lime and can dart away from a nearby cast lure.

How it works

The manifest supplies single-frame PNGs and stats for fish that inherit the base FishSmall and FishMedium objects. The generated initializer registers four Goldies in x = 1,100–2,200 and y = 1,400–1,900, and three Glowfins in x = 1,400–2,800 and y = 1,600–2,300.

Glowfin's Create hook randomizes its glow phase and initializes a dart cooldown. Its End Step hook runs after inherited fish logic. At night it pulses the sprite tint and adds a velocity burst away from a cast lure within 120 pixels while the fish is swimming and the cooldown has expired. During the day it dims and sinks while shallow.
