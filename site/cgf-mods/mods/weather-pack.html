What it does

Weather Pack brings Super Sunny, Rainy, Windy and Fog to the legacy daylight cycle. Each weather has its own sky, sound and two surface fish. A Lightbulb lure near the surface thins the fog.

Controls

  • Open the JAMM Console in the fishing world with F12. Type weather to list the installed weather IDs.
  • Use weather super-sunny, weather rainy, weather windy or weather fog to request a weather. Qualify a shared short ID as weather weather-pack:fog. A daytime request made at night waits for daylight.
  • Use weather clear for clear skies or weather auto to resume the forecast.

How it works

JAMM compiles the four weather declarations into one persistent obj_jamm_weather registry. The controller draws a weighted forecast from the slot seed, the current local clock hour and sorted weather IDs. The same slot and hour resume the stored forecast. The next hour produces a new draw, and the legacy night predicate still controls when a daytime weather can run.

Each weather registers two species with the Fishalog and populates a school near the cat's dock or boat position using free surface-water positions. Super Sunny brings Bask and Flaret, Rainy brings Drizzit and Lowbelly, Windy brings Kest and Tack, and Fog brings Mote and Veilmouth. While these schools are active, the controller parks eligible vanilla surface fish. At clear or night it restores the vanilla fish and retires free weather fish while allowing catches in progress to finish.

The sky and fish recipes sample the player's legacy sprite frames during the build and add original SVG parts. The runtime draws tinted parallax sky layers behind the landscape and foreground haze and particles above it. Repeating layers wrap horizontally across the camera, with wrapped atlas padding for filtering and matching edges on the two Fog bands.

The runtime blends weather music and ambience with the native surface track and applies weather settings to the sea tint and waves. Fog's Step hook lowers the opacity of foreground layers and particles when a native Lightbulb is near the surface.
