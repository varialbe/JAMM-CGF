What it does

JAMM Console adds a keyboard-operated overlay for testing progression and equipment in the legacy game. You can grant gear, set money and level, or fill the Fishalog from the command prompt.

Controls

  • Press F12 or backtick to toggle the console. Enter submits, Up and Down browse history, and Escape closes it.
  • help lists commands, clear clears the log, and close or exit closes the overlay.
  • unlockgear unlocks equipment, upgrades, hats, HUD menus and nightfall. unlockall also maxes your level and fills the Fishalog.
  • level <n> sets your level and maxlevel uses the game's maximum. catalog all or unlockfish marks the fish caught.
  • money <n> sets your dollars and money +<n> adds dollars.

How it works

The generator places obj_jamm_console in NewWorld and room11. Its Begin Step processes the input buffer, normalizes commands and consumes gameplay keys while the overlay is open. Its End Step maintains the open overlay's rod swing lock and Draw GUI renders the prompt and log.

Gear unlocks iterate the live item tables, so their row counts can include gear from the loaded profile. Fishalog commands update the live catalog arrays. Level and money commands clamp input and write to the player's current state.

The controller tracks the active SaveManager instance and slot to reset its unlock bookkeeping on a slot change. Console commands that alter progress print a save warning.
