What it does

Submarine adds an optional boat to the Boat shop for $125M. Take it east of the dock, press V to dive and steer through open water with the arrow keys or WASD. A bow headlight lights the way, a depth readout and an oxygen meter (110 seconds, refilled at the surface) sit in the corner, and you can cast and reel from the bow. Version 0.1.0 is a first playable preview.

Requirements

  • JAMM-CGF with a configured August 2023 legacy VM base.
  • Build into a separate game copy; do not use the current Steam YYC archive as the base.

Install

  • Download this source ZIP.
  • In JAMM, choose Add mods and import the ZIP.
  • Enable it in a profile, check the profile, then build or install to a separate game copy.

In-game controls

  • V (gamepad Y / Triangle): dive or surface.
  • Arrow keys or WASD (D-pad or left stick): steer underwater.
  • L (X / Square): toggle the headlight.
  • Hold and release Space or the left mouse button (RB / R1): cast from the bow; hold to reel.
  • Q at the surface: vanilla swimming.

How it works

When the submarine is equipped and below the sea line, a controller takes over movement from the boat, applies buoyancy and a hull depth limit of 4,800 units, and counts down oxygen. Running out of oxygen starts an emergency ascent.

The bow cast reuses the vanilla rod and lure, so bait, bites, catches, selling and Fishalog progress work as usual. Reel in before moving or surfacing.

FAQ

Where is the submarine in the shop?

In the Boat shop for $125M. Scroll the shop if the card is on a later page.

Why can't I dive at the dock?

Dive in open water east of the dock and steer around rock before casting.
