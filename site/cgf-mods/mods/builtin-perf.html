What it does

JAMM Perf reduces work in fish collision, avoidance and scenery updates in the legacy game. Its experimental mode also reduces how often eligible distant fish run part of their logic.

Controls

  • Press F8 to switch between mode 0 (base optimizations) and mode 1 (base optimizations plus experimental scheduling).

How it works

The generator guards light updates on Magmer and Volteen against stale instance IDs. It skips the precise deep-rock collision check and trawler net collision query in fish states that cannot use their results.

Fish avoidance computes two fixed expressions once per call and retains the original neighbour traversal. Scenery caches its depth transform per instance and still updates its parallax position each tick.

The obj_jamm_perf controller configures an early-out in the base Fish End Step. The scheduler checks ancestry, fish state and distances to active views, lures, swimmers, boats and nets before skipping work. Eligible instances are staggered by instance ID and tick, and their engine alarms, motion and Draw events still run.
