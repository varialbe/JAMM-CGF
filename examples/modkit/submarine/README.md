# Submarine 0.1.0

Enable this optional ModKit package in a JAMM profile built against the August 2023 legacy VM version of Cat Goes Fishing. The Boat shop sells the Submarine for $125M. It holds 12 fish. Scroll the shop if the card is on a later page.

| Action | Keyboard or mouse | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dive or surface | V | Y / Triangle |
| Steer underwater | Arrows or WASD | D-pad or left stick |
| Toggle the headlight | L | X / Square |
| Cast from the bow | Hold and release Space or left mouse | Hold and release RB / R1 |
| Reel | Hold Space or left mouse | Hold RB / R1 |

Take the sub east of the dock, then dive in open water. The oxygen meter gives you 110 seconds and refills at the surface. The starter hull stops at 4,800 units below the sea line. Steer around rock before casting. While the lure is out, reel in before moving or surfacing. Running out of oxygen starts an emergency ascent.

The game saves ownership and the equipped boat. A new session starts at the surface. Use Q at the surface for vanilla swimming.

The source package contains SVG details and sprite recipes. JAMM creates the hull and the shop card from locally sourced Tech Boat parts when it builds the package.
